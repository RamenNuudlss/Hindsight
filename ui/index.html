Hindsight

Keeps the last few minutes of your game in memory. Press Alt+F10 and they're saved as a clip.

  • Installs OBS if it isn't on your PC, and gives it a profile of its own. Your other OBS profiles stay as they are.
  • Starts OBS quietly in the tray when you sign in, recording into memory, not disk.
  • Uses your OBS as it is. Your profile, scenes and projectors stay. Hindsight turns on OBS's replay buffer and sets its length.
  • Opens OBS when you sign in and keeps the last few minutes of your live scene in memory, not on disk.
  • Turns off Xbox Game Bar capture and OBS's exit warning so they don't get in the way.
  • Remove puts it all back. Your clips and OBS itself stay.

Good to know before you start
  • Game capture hooks into games, and a few anti-cheats object to that. For Valorant, FACEIT and similar, switch to Whole screen in Capture.
  • Whole screen records everything on that display, notifications included.
  • Your microphone is recorded unless you turn it off in Audio.
  • Protected video, like streaming services, shows up black. Hindsight doesn't get around DRM.
  • If OBS crashes, Hindsight restarts it and skips OBS's safe-mode question.
  • An outdated OBS plugin can show a load error when OBS starts. Clicking OK is fine.
